1. Privacy Policy (GDPR, CCPA & CalOPPA Compliant)

Last updated: October 2026. Nitro Apex is engineered with privacy-by-design principles.

Data Collection & Minimization

Nitro Apex collects the minimum data required to deliver the gameplay experience:

  • Driver Display Name: Saved exclusively in your local browser storage (localStorage). Never sold, rented, or monetized.
  • Vehicle & Paint Preferences: Stored locally in your client browser.
  • Multiplayer Ephemeral Coordinates: Sent across encrypted WebSockets (WSS) in real-time at 20Hz solely to synchronize car positions during active race sessions. This data is never permanently logged to databases.

Third-Party Analytics & Tracking

We employ zero third-party tracking scripts, zero advertising pixels (Facebook Pixel, TikTok pixel), and zero behavioral profiling tools.

Your Rights (GDPR Art. 15-22 / CCPA)

You have the right to inspect, export, or immediately purge all data associated with your gameplay. Use the Data Deletion option in the footer to purge all stored keys instantly.
